## S-20261011-002

- Vietnamese: Tôi sẽ xem lại tài liệu và phản hồi cho bạn vào sáng mai.
- First learner answer: i will review the documents and reply to you on tomorrow
- Standard version: I’ll review the documents and get back to you tomorrow morning.
- Assessment: incorrect
- Observed explanation/errors: `On tomorrow` is not idiomatic; use `tomorrow morning` without `on`. The response also omitted `morning`.
- Structures/collocations: review the documents; get back to someone; tomorrow morning
- Topic: work – follow-up replies
- First-study date: 2026-10-11
- Memory status: learning

## S-20261011-003

- Vietnamese: Cuộc họp đã được dời sang chiều thứ Năm.
- First learner answer: all the meetings have been moved to thursday afternoon
- Standard version: The meeting has been moved to Thursday afternoon.
- Assessment: incorrect
- Observed explanation/errors: Changed the source from one meeting to all meetings. `Has been moved` is a natural passive form for the singular source sentence.
- Structures/collocations: meeting has been moved; Thursday afternoon
- Topic: work – meetings and scheduling
- First-study date: 2026-10-11
- Memory status: learning
